## What breast augmentation involves (high level)

Breast augmentation increases breast volume using implants or, in selected cases, fat transfer. Implant choice involves fill material, shape, profile, and size matched to chest width, tissue thickness, and aesthetic goals. Placement (for example, above or below the muscle) and incision location are clinical decisions—not catalogue picks.

A thorough consult reviews medical history, pregnancy plans, mammogram needs when relevant, skin quality, asymmetry, and lifestyle. The goal is a proportionate plan you understand, including trade-offs and limitations. No reputable surgeon can promise a cloned photo result.

## Credentials and facility checks that reduce guesswork

Ask and verify:

• Plastic surgery specialty training and active professional registration
• SCCP or equivalent society membership when claimed—confirm independently
• Hospital or surgical-center accreditation and emergency support
• Implant manufacturer, model, warranty documentation, and device tracking
• Pre-op imaging or sizing method used in your case
• Scheduled post-op visits before flight clearance and remote follow-up afterward

Discuss general risks associated with breast implants and surgery: bleeding, infection, capsular contracture, implant malposition or rupture over time, changes in sensation, asymmetry, anesthesia risks, and the possibility of future revision. Transparency here is a quality signal.

## Recovery themes to confirm with your surgeon

Early recovery often includes a support bra or garment as directed, limited overhead reaching, short walks, and sleep positioning guidance. Swelling and settling (“drop and fluff” language you may see online) are gradual; final appearance takes months. Return to desk work, driving, exercise, and underwire bras should follow written clinical instructions—not influencer schedules.

Ask how mammograms and future breast imaging are handled after implants, and how you should monitor for changes once you are back in the United States.

## Smart questions for a breast augmentation consult

• Based on my measurements, which implant dimensions are realistic—and which are not?
• Implant vs. fat transfer: which fits my tissue and goals?
• Where would scars typically sit, and how are they followed?
• What is the plan if a complication appears after I fly home?
• How many days should I remain in Cartagena before considering travel?
• Who is my after-hours contact while I am still in Colombia?
