A [BBL in Colombia](/en/procedures/bbl/) search usually mixes two decisions: whether gluteal fat grafting abroad can be planned safely, and how long recovery and a Cartagena stay actually take. On the recovery side, the American Society of Plastic Surgeons (ASPS) advises avoiding prolonged sitting for about **two weeks** after fat grafting—some surgeons prefer longer—and Cleveland Clinic describes no sitting or lying on the buttocks for at least two weeks, with a specialized pillow often used for weeks after that. On the flight side, Avianca lists **10 days** after buttock fat injection, **14 days** after liposuction, and **15 days** when two or more body procedures are combined, plus a medical certificate issued within 10 days of travel. Your surgeon’s clearance still comes first.

## How long does BBL recovery take?

A Brazilian butt lift combines liposuction from donor areas with fat grafting to the buttocks and hips. Recovery therefore covers **two** healing zones: the liposuction sites and the grafted buttocks.

Patient-education timelines that are useful for planning (not personal clearance):

| Stage | What general sources describe |
|---|---|
| First days | Support garment around the clock as ASPS describes; short walks; sleep face down or on your side |
| About 2 weeks | ASPS: avoid prolonged sitting (some surgeons longer). Cleveland Clinic: no sitting or lying on the buttocks for at least two weeks |
| ~10–14 days | Cleveland Clinic: many people return to work in this window, with desk jobs needing sitting workarounds |
| ~6–8 weeks | ASPS: exercise can generally resume as cleared by the surgeon |
| Up to ~1 year | ASPS: after fat transfer, final results can take up to a year as some grafted fat is reabsorbed |

Cleveland Clinic puts fuller recovery in the **two-to-three-month** range for many patients, while still noting that individual timelines vary. None of these day counts replaces the written plan your surgical team sets after examining you.

## How long should you stay in Colombia after a BBL?

There is no single “right” hotel booking for every traveler. Build the calendar from three floors, then add margin:

1. **Airline boarding rules** if you fly Avianca (or read your carrier’s policy if you do not).
2. **Early recovery needs**—sitting limits, garment, companion help, and in-person checks.
3. **Your surgeon’s clearance** after seeing how you are healing.

### Avianca lines that shape a BBL itinerary

From Avianca’s [medical-conditions travel page](https://ayuda.avianca.com/hc/en-us/articles/13090597036315-Are-there-any-travel-restrictions-for-passengers-with-special-medical-conditions):

| Item | Avianca published minimum |
|---|---|
| Buttock fat injection | 10 days |
| Liposuction | 14 days |
| Two or more body procedures combined | 15 days (depending on recovery) |
| Medical certificate | Issued no earlier than 10 days before travel |

Because a BBL harvests fat with liposuction, your certificate may describe more than buttock fat injection, and that decides whether the 10-, 14- or 15-day line applies to you. Ask which procedures it will list. If the airport health authority disagrees with your doctor about the days needed, Avianca says the health authority’s opinion prevails.

Practical trip math for many U.S. patients: arrival days for evaluation and labs **plus** at least the longest Avianca line that applies **plus** a buffer for a later clearance. Keep the return ticket changeable. ## Sitting and sleeping after a BBL

Pressure on newly grafted fat is the recovery detail that makes travel planning feel different from many other body procedures.

**Planning checklist (general education):**

- **Prolonged sitting:** ASPS asks patients to avoid it for about two weeks after fat grafting; some surgeons prefer longer.
- **How to sit when you must:** ASPS describes a cushion behind the legs (hamstrings) so the buttocks stay off the seat.
- **Sleep:** ASPS encourages face-down or side sleeping. The Colombian Society of Plastic Surgery (SCCP) patient page on [aumento de glúteos](https://cirugiaplastica.org.co/procedimientos/aumento-de-gluteos/) similarly notes sleeping face down in the first week after lipoinjection and avoiding hard sitting surfaces.
- **Pillow phase:** Cleveland Clinic describes short sits on a specialized pillow after several weeks, and sitting without that pillow for roughly eight weeks in their educational timeline.
- **Garment:** ASPS describes a support garment worn around the clock; duration is surgeon-directed.
- **Companion help:** Getting in and out of bed, meals, and early walks are easier with help—especially in the first days.

These are general guidelines from surgical societies and hospital patient education. The written instructions from your own surgery are what you follow.

## When can you fly home after a BBL?

Feeling better and being cleared to fly are two different things. Surgery and long flights each raise the risk of blood clots, and ASPS lists blood clots among the risks of buttock enhancement, so the flight date deserves the same planning as the surgery date.

For the full Avianca table, certificate window, and clot-prevention basics on the plane, use the dedicated guide to [flying after plastic surgery](/blog/flying-after-plastic-surgery/). Here the focus is why sitting limits and stay length belong in the same conversation as the flight date.

Before you lock a return:

- Schedule the pre-flight visit and certificate inside Avianca’s 10-day window.
- Ask how you should sit during the flight (cushion, aisle seat, walking breaks).
- Ask who answers if something changes after you land in the United States.

**Red flags in a travel plan:** a return flight bought before surgery is scheduled; a package that assumes you fly within a few days; nobody available to examine you before departure; vague answers about sitting after grafting.

## Safety questions that still come first

Recovery planning only matters if the operative plan is credible. ASPS, The Aesthetic Society, and their research foundations’ [2022 joint safety statement](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/for-medical-professionals/publications/psn-extra/news/gluteal-fat-grafting-a-joint-safety-statement) supports injecting fat **only into the subcutaneous space, above the gluteal fascia**, and using **real-time ultrasound** to track the cannula tip. It also stresses licensed facilities, surgeon engagement before and after surgery, and availability for complications whether the patient is local or has traveled. ISAPS has likewise endorsed patient-safety guidance on gluteal fat grafting.

Bring these into any consult:

1. How do you keep fat in the subcutaneous plane, and do you use ultrasound guidance?
2. Where is surgery performed, who provides anesthesia, and what emergency support exists?
3. How is clot risk assessed for someone flying internationally afterward?
4. What is the follow-up plan after I am already home?

ASPS lists risks of buttock enhancement that include bleeding, infection, fat necrosis, contour issues, blood clots, and other complications; fat embolism is the safety topic that made society statements so specific about injection plane. A marketing page that skips risks is a weak quality signal.

## What a consult decides before you book flights

Strong candidacy conversations usually cover stable weight, realistic goals, enough donor fat, smoking status, clotting history, medications, and prior surgeries. If major weight change is still underway, or if you cannot arrange help during early recovery, many teams prefer to wait.

Also ask whether your goals are better served by fat grafting, liposuction-focused contouring, or a staged plan. A blog cannot declare you a candidate; photos and history only start the conversation.

### Credentials worth verifying in Colombia

1. Confirm plastic surgery specialty registration (ReTHUS).
2. If SCCP membership is claimed, verify it in the [SCCP directory](https://www.cirugiaplastica.org.co/).
3. Ask for the exact facility name and check that surgical services are authorized for that site (REPS).
4. Confirm anesthesiologist coverage and a real operating room—not an improvised office.
5. Get a written outline of in-person checks before flight clearance and how remote follow-up works once you are home.

## Frequently asked questions

**How long is BBL recovery time?**
ASPS describes about two weeks without prolonged sitting after fat grafting (some surgeons longer), garments as directed, and exercise often around six to eight weeks. Cleveland Clinic describes fuller recovery over roughly two to three months for many people, with final fat-transfer results still settling later. Your surgeon sets your plan.

**How long should I stay in Colombia after a BBL?**
Plan around the longest Avianca line that applies to your certificate (often 14 or 15 days when liposuction or combined body procedures are involved), plus arrival days and a buffer for clearance. Do not treat a blog’s itinerary as authorization to fly.

**When can I sit after a BBL?**
ASPS advises avoiding prolonged sitting for about two weeks and using a thigh cushion when sitting is unavoidable. Cleveland Clinic’s education describes at least two weeks off the buttocks and a longer pillow phase. Follow your written instructions.

**When can I fly after a BBL?**
Avianca lists 10 days after buttock fat injection and 14 after liposuction; combined body procedures can fall under 15 days. See [flying after plastic surgery](/blog/flying-after-plastic-surgery/) for the full table and certificate rules.

**Is a BBL in Colombia safe?**
Safety depends on surgeon, facility, and technique—not the country name. Society guidance emphasizes subcutaneous-only grafting with ultrasound confirmation, licensed facilities, and real follow-up for travelers.

**How long until final BBL results?**
ASPS notes that after fat transfer it can take up to a year to see final results because some grafted fat is reabsorbed. Weight changes later can still change buttock volume.
