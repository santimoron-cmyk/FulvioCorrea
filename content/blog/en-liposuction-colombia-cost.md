## Four names, four different plans

"Lipo" is used for several different plans. Before comparing any quote, match the name to what you actually want done.

| What you are looking for | What it is usually called | How it is quoted in Cartagena |
|---|---|---|
| Less fat in one or a few areas, without detailed definition | Liposuction | Quoted per case after the consultation |
| Shaping and definition of the contour | HD liposculpture (Liposurg) | Quoted per case. A different operation from simple liposuction |
| Arms or legs added to a liposuction | Arm or leg liposuction, as an add-on | Quoted with the main plan. More areas change operating time |
| Retraction technology, when the plan uses it | Retraction technology, per area | Quoted only if the plan uses it |
| Abdomen, waist and back in one session | Lipo 360 | See [lipo 360 cost in Colombia](/blog/lipo-360-colombia-cost/) |
| Fat moved to the buttocks and hips | BBL (Brazilian butt lift) | Quoted per case, see [BBL cost in Colombia](/blog/bbl-colombia-cost/) |
| Fat plus loose skin on the lower abdomen | Lipoabdominoplasty | Quoted per case. Not the same operation as liposuction |
| Consultation, virtual or in person | — | USD 150 / COP 400,000 |

The consultation fee is not a surgery price. Adding plans together in your head is not the price of a combined operation, which is quoted as a whole. [Book a valuation](/book-consultation/) for the written quote.

The real quote always comes after the virtual or in-person consultation. It does not commit the doctor to operate.

## Why a price "per area" online rarely matches

Search for how much lipo costs in Colombia and you will find tables by number of areas, by city and by technique, in pesos and in dollars. A number only compares with another if it describes the same plan. Three questions settle that:

1. **Which areas, exactly?** "One area" can mean the lower abdomen alone or the whole abdomen with the flanks. Ask for the list of areas in writing.
2. **Volume or definition?** Liposuction to reduce volume and HD liposculpture to define the contour are different operations with different planning. A simple liposuction quote is not the price of an HD plan, and a low "lipo" price is usually not the price of an HD plan.
3. **What is inside the number?** Anesthesia, the operating room, tests before surgery, the compression garment and visits after surgery are in some prices and missing from others.

Mayo Clinic adds a fact that explains part of the spread: if only a small amount of fat is removed, liposuction may be done in a clinic or medical office, while a large amount of fat, or other procedures at the same time, may move the surgery to a hospital. Where you are operated on, and with which anesthesia, is part of what you pay.

## Can one small area cost less?

It can, and that is exactly why it has no fixed published price: a single area is quoted per case. The figures that do exist for single areas are the arm and leg rows, and they apply as **add-ons to a liposuction**, not as standalone surgeries. The same applies to smaller areas such as the chin and neck, which Mayo Clinic lists among the areas liposuction can treat; the plan and the price come out of the consultation.

What a smaller plan does not change is the fixed part of the budget: the consultation, tests before surgery and the nights you stay in Cartagena before you can fly.

## What liposuction does not fix, and why that changes the budget

Some quotes go up or down because the plan changes once the surgeon examines you. According to Mayo Clinic:

- **Liposuction is not a weight-loss method.** It works best for someone with fat in specific areas and an otherwise stable weight. Results usually last if your weight stays the same.
- **It does not tighten loose skin.** If your skin is thin and not elastic, the treated area may look loose. When there is excess skin on the lower abdomen, the conversation moves to [tummy tuck or liposuction](/blog/tummy-tuck-vs-liposuction/) and to a lipoabdominoplasty, which has its own range.
- **It does not remove cellulite or stretch marks.** A quote that promises it should be questioned.
- **The risk rises with larger areas and combined procedures.** Mayo Clinic notes that complications are more likely when the surgeon works on larger body surfaces or does several procedures in the same operation. That is a medical reason, not only a price reason, to plan the areas carefully.

## The nights in Cartagena belong in the same budget

The price of the surgery is one part. The other is how long you stay, and for liposuction the sources give clear floors:

- **The American Society of Plastic Surgeons (ASPS)** suggests waiting 5 to 7 days before flying after body procedures such as liposuction.
- **Avianca**, Colombia's flag carrier, lists 14 days after liposuction and 15 if two or more body procedures are combined, and asks for a medical certificate issued no more than 10 days before the flight.

Mayo Clinic also notes that most people wear compression garments for a few weeks, may need a few days before going back to work, and a few weeks before exercise. Lodging for those nights, meals, local transport and a companion go in the same spreadsheet as the quote. ## How to ask for a quote you can compare

1. Send front, side and back photos and say which areas bother you; the [international patients](/international-patients/) program starts this way.
2. Ask whether your plan is liposuction or HD liposculpture, and which areas are included.
3. Ask which items are inside the number: surgeon, anesthesia, facility, tests, garment and visits.
4. Ask for the currency and, in pesos, the exchange-rate date.
5. Check who operates: the [ReTHUS registry](https://www.minsalud.gov.co/salud/PO/paginas/registro-unico-nacional-del-talento-humano-en-salud-rethus.aspx) and, if membership is claimed, the [SCCP surgeon search](https://cirugiaplastica.org.co/buscar-cirujano/).

**How much does lipo cost in Colombia with Dr. Fulvio Correa?**
There is no published surgery price. HD liposculpture and liposuction that is not HD are both quoted per case after the consultation, because the areas, the technique and the facility change the plan.

**How much does it cost to add arms or legs?**
There is no published add-on price. Arms or legs are quoted with the main plan, because each extra area changes operating time, anesthesia and recovery.

**Is there a price for liposuction of just one area?**
Not a fixed one. A single area is quoted per case, because the amount of fat, the technique and where the surgery is done change from one patient to another.

**Why are some lipo prices in Colombia much lower?**
Usually because they describe a different plan: fewer areas, volume reduction instead of HD definition, or a number without anesthesia, facility, tests or follow-up. Compare item by item.
