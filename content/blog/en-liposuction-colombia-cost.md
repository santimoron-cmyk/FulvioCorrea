## What changes the cost of liposuction

"Lipo" is used for several different plans. Match the name to the operation before you compare any quote.

| What you are looking for | What it is usually called | How it is quoted in Cartagena |
|---|---|---|
| Less fat in one or a few areas, without detailed definition | Liposuction | Quoted per case after the consultation |
| Shaping and definition of the contour | HD liposculpture (Liposurg) | Quoted per case. A different operation from simple liposuction |
| Arms or legs added to a liposuction | Arm or leg liposuction, as part of the plan | Quoted with the main plan. More areas change operating time |
| Retraction technology, when the plan uses it | Retraction technology | Quoted only if the plan uses it |
| Abdomen, waist and back in one session | Lipo 360 | See [lipo 360 cost in Colombia](/blog/lipo-360-colombia-cost/) |
| Fat moved to the buttocks and hips | BBL (Brazilian butt lift) | Quoted per case. See [what a BBL quote includes](/blog/bbl-colombia-cost/) |
| Fat plus loose skin on the lower abdomen | Lipoabdominoplasty | Quoted per case. Not the same operation as liposuction |
| Consultation, virtual or in person | — | USD 150 / COP 400,000 |

Three questions settle whether two numbers describe the same plan:

1. **Which areas, exactly?** "One area" can mean the lower abdomen alone or the whole abdomen with the flanks. Ask for the list in writing.
2. **Volume or definition?** Liposuction to reduce volume and HD liposculpture to define the contour are different operations. A simple liposuction quote is not the quote for an HD plan.
3. **What is inside the number?** Anesthesia, the operating room, tests, the garment and visits after surgery are in some quotes and missing from others.

Mayo Clinic adds a fact that explains part of the difference: if only a small amount of fat is removed, liposuction may be done in a clinic or office, while a large amount of fat, or other procedures at the same time, may move the surgery to a hospital. Where you are operated on, and with which anesthesia, is part of the plan. A single area can be a smaller operation, and that is why it is still quoted per case. Chin and neck are among the areas Mayo Clinic lists. The plan still comes out of the consultation.

## What liposuction does not fix

Some quotes change because the plan changes once the surgeon examines you. According to Mayo Clinic:

- **Liposuction is not a weight-loss method.** It works best for someone with fat in specific areas and an otherwise stable weight. Results usually last if your weight stays the same.
- **It does not tighten loose skin.** If the skin is thin and not elastic, the treated area may look loose. When there is excess skin on the lower abdomen, the conversation moves to [tummy tuck or liposuction](/blog/tummy-tuck-vs-liposuction/) and to a lipoabdominoplasty, which is quoted on its own.
- **It does not remove cellulite or stretch marks.** A quote that promises either should be questioned.
- **The risk rises with larger areas and combined procedures.** Mayo Clinic notes that complications are more likely when the surgeon works on larger body surfaces or does several procedures in the same operation.

## What the quote includes

If a line is not written in the quote, treat it as not included until someone confirms it in writing.

## How to compare quotes and red flags

1. Send front, side and back photos and say which areas bother you. The [international patients](/international-patients/) program starts this way.
2. Ask whether the plan is liposuction or HD liposculpture, and which areas are included.
3. Line up surgeon, anesthesia, facility, tests, garment and visits. A lower number that leaves those out is not the same quote.
4. Ask for the currency and, in pesos, the exchange-rate date.
5. Check who operates: the [ReTHUS registry](https://www.minsalud.gov.co/salud/PO/paginas/registro-unico-nacional-del-talento-humano-en-salud-rethus.aspx) and, if membership is claimed, the [SCCP surgeon search](https://cirugiaplastica.org.co/buscar-cirujano/). Ask for the facility's name and whether it is authorized for surgery.
6. Ask what happens if you need another visit before you are cleared to fly.

## Frequently asked questions

**How much does liposuction cost in Colombia?**
There is no published surgery price. HD liposculpture and liposuction that is not HD are both quoted after a valuation, because the areas, the technique and the facility change the plan.

**What does a liposuction quote include?**
Ask it to name the areas and whether the plan is HD, plus anesthesia, the clinic or hospital, lab tests, the garment, medication, follow-up visits and any prescribed post-op care.

**Is there a price for liposuction of one area?**
Not a published one. A single area is quoted per case, because the amount of fat, the technique and where the surgery is done change from one patient to another.

**Why are some lipo quotes much lower?**
Usually because they describe a different plan: fewer areas, volume reduction instead of HD definition, or a number that leaves out anesthesia, the facility, tests or follow-up. Compare line by line.

**How much is the consultation?**
USD 150 / COP 400,000, virtual or in person. It is not a surgery price.

**How many days should I budget in Cartagena?**
At least 14 days after liposuction if you fly Avianca, or 15 if body procedures are combined, plus the days before surgery. Your surgeon confirms the date after examining you.
