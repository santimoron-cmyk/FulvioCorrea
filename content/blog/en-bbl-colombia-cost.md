## Why a BBL has no fixed price

The American Society of Plastic Surgeons (ASPS) describes a BBL as fat harvested by liposuction, purified and injected into the buttocks, and notes that surgeons often harvest up to three times more fat than they finally inject. That single sentence explains most of the variation in a BBL quote in Colombia:

- **Donor areas.** Abdomen and flanks only, or abdomen, flanks and back, or thighs as well. More donor areas mean more operating time.
- **Available fat.** A very lean patient may have little fat to move, which limits volume and can change the plan entirely. Sometimes liposuction alone, or [lipo 360](/blog/lipo-360-colombia-cost/), is the better plan.
- **What else is combined.** A tummy tuck or breast surgery in the same trip changes anesthesia time, the facility stay and the recovery, and is quoted as a whole.
- **Safety requirements.** The precautions described below take time and equipment. They are part of the price, not an extra.
- **Your health history.** Tests or clearances your surgeon asks for before surgery.

So the honest answer to "how much is a BBL in Colombia?" is a written quote for your plan, not a number on a social media post.

## The U.S. benchmark, and what it does not include

ASPS publishes surgeon/physician fee ranges reported by its members. For 2024, buttock augmentation with fat grafting is listed at **USD 7,000–11,500**, and ASPS member surgeons reported **29,466** of these procedures that year. ASPS explains that the range is a projection built from averages submitted by surveyed members across different regions and practice settings.

That figure is the surgeon's fee. A total bill also includes anesthesia, the operating facility, tests, garments and follow-up, so comparing it with a full quote from anywhere is not comparing like with like. This page does not claim a percentage saving.

## The cheapest BBL quote is a safety question

Price pressure is where BBL safety problems start. In their 2022 [joint safety statement](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/for-medical-professionals/publications/psn-extra/news/gluteal-fat-grafting-a-joint-safety-statement), ASPS, The Aesthetic Society and their research foundations support injecting fat **only into the subcutaneous space, above the gluteal muscle**, using **real-time ultrasound** to follow the cannula, operating only in licensed facilities, not delegating critical steps to untrained staff, and keeping the surgeon available for complications whether the patient is local or has traveled. ISAPS has endorsed [patient-safety guidance on gluteal fat grafting](https://www.isaps.org/media/3eukkm04/230214-endorsed-patient-safety-gluteal-fat-grafting.pdf) along the same lines.

Each of those points costs time or equipment. When a BBL quote is far below others for the same plan, ask:

1. Where exactly will the fat be placed, and how is that plane confirmed during surgery?
2. Who performs each step of the operation?
3. In which facility, and is it authorized for surgery? In Colombia, facilities are registered in the health ministry's provider registry (REPS).
4. Is the surgeon a registered plastic surgeon? In Colombia, check the [ReTHUS registry](https://www.minsalud.gov.co/salud/PO/paginas/registro-unico-nacional-del-talento-humano-en-salud-rethus.aspx) and, if membership is claimed, the [Colombian Society of Plastic Surgery (SCCP)](https://cirugiaplastica.org.co/).
5. What happens, and who pays, if you need extra care before flying home?

The full checklist for comparing surgeons is in [how to choose a plastic surgeon in Colombia](/best-plastic-surgeon-colombia/). The ASPS [briefing paper on cosmetic surgery tourism](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/news/briefing-papers/briefing-paper-cosmetic-surgery-tourism) gives the same advice for any trip: verify credentials and plan follow-up before you travel.

## The trip costs more days for a BBL than for most procedures

The surgery quote is only part of the budget. A BBL changes the trip in two specific ways:

- **Sitting.** ASPS advises avoiding prolonged sitting for about two weeks after gluteal fat grafting, and some surgeons prefer longer. A long flight works against that.
- **Flight rules.** Avianca, Colombia's flag carrier, lists 10 days after buttock fat injection, 14 days after liposuction and 15 days when two or more body procedures are combined, with a medical certificate issued no more than 10 days before travel. A BBL includes liposuction, so ask which procedures your certificate will list.

That means budgeting lodging, meals and local transport for a longer stay, plus a changeable return ticket. Some patients arrange an equipped [recovery house in Cartagena](/recovery-house-cartagena/) with nursing support; that plan is tailored at the consultation. The day-by-day planning is in [how long to stay after a BBL](/blog/bbl-colombia/) and [how many days before flying after surgery](/blog/flying-after-plastic-surgery/).
