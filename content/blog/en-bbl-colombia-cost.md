## What changes the cost of a BBL

The American Society of Plastic Surgeons (ASPS) describes a BBL as fat taken by liposuction, purified, and injected into the buttocks, and notes that surgeons often harvest up to three times more fat than they finally inject. That is why two people asking the same question can need two different operations:

- **Donor areas.** Abdomen and flanks only, or abdomen, flanks and back, or thighs as well. More areas mean more operating time.
- **Available fat.** A very lean patient may have little fat to move. That can limit volume and can change the plan. Sometimes liposuction alone, or [lipo 360](/blog/lipo-360-colombia-cost/), is the better operation.
- **What else is combined.** A tummy tuck or breast surgery in the same trip changes anesthesia time, the facility stay and the recovery. The combination is quoted as one plan.
- **Safety steps.** The precautions below take time and equipment. They belong in the plan, not as an optional add-on.
- **Your health history.** Labs or clearances your surgeon asks for before surgery.

ASPS member surgeons reported **29,466** buttock augmentations with fat grafting in 2024. ASPS publishes a surgeon's fee and discusses anesthesia and the operating room separately. A surgeon's fee is not a total bill. This page does not reprint those U.S. figures and does not claim a percentage saving. Compare one full written quote with another.

## What the quote includes

A useful BBL quote names each service. If a line is not written down, treat it as not included until someone confirms it in writing.

| Item | What the line should say |
|---|---|
| Surgeon | Which donor areas, and whether grafting is to the buttocks only or to the buttocks and hips |
| Anesthesia | ASPS notes a BBL is done with intravenous sedation or general anesthesia. Who the anesthesiologist is, and whether that fee is in the quote |
| Clinic or hospital | Where the surgery is done, whether the facility is authorized for surgery, and whether an overnight stay is included if the plan needs one |
| Lab tests | Which studies are required, and whether they are in the quote |
| Garments | Whether the first compression garment is included, and whether you will need a BBL cushion |
| Medication | What is provided for the first days after surgery |
| Follow-up visits | How many visits happen in Cartagena, and how follow-up works after you fly home |
| Post-op care | Lymphatic drainage or massage only if the surgeon prescribes it, and whether that care is in the quote or arranged separately |
| Currency | USD or COP, and the exchange-rate date if the quote is in pesos |

Flights, lodging, meals, a companion and time away from work sit outside the surgical quote. Some patients arrange an equipped [recovery house in Cartagena](/recovery-house-cartagena/) with nursing, transfers and supplies. That plan is set at the valuation and has no package price.

## How to compare quotes and red flags

In their 2022 [joint safety statement](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/for-medical-professionals/publications/psn-extra/news/gluteal-fat-grafting-a-joint-safety-statement), ASPS, The Aesthetic Society and their research foundations support injecting fat **only into the subcutaneous space, above the gluteal muscle**, using **real-time ultrasound** to follow the cannula, operating only in licensed facilities, not delegating critical steps to untrained staff, and keeping the surgeon available if a complication occurs, whether you live in Cartagena or you traveled. ISAPS has endorsed [patient-safety guidance on gluteal fat grafting](https://www.isaps.org/media/3eukkm04/230214-endorsed-patient-safety-gluteal-fat-grafting.pdf) along the same lines.

Line the quotes up before you compare them. A lower number is only useful if it describes the same plan.

1. **Same operation.** Donor areas, grafting sites, and any combined procedure should match. Gluteal implants are a different operation and are also quoted per case. See the [BBL page](/procedures/bbl/).
2. **Fat plane.** Where will the fat be placed, and how is that plane confirmed during surgery?
3. **Who operates.** Who performs each step? In Colombia, check the [ReTHUS registry](https://www.minsalud.gov.co/salud/PO/paginas/registro-unico-nacional-del-talento-humano-en-salud-rethus.aspx). If membership is claimed, check the [Colombian Society of Plastic Surgery (SCCP)](https://cirugiaplastica.org.co/).
4. **Where.** The facility's name and address, in writing, and whether it is authorized for surgical services. In Colombia, facilities are registered in the health ministry's provider registry (REPS). The consultation office and the surgical clinic are often different addresses.
5. **Missing lines.** Anesthesia, the facility, tests, garments or follow-up left out of the cheaper quote.
6. **If something goes wrong before you fly.** Who is available, and who pays for extra care, if you need it before you are cleared to travel.

The longer checklist is in [how to choose a plastic surgeon in Colombia](/best-plastic-surgeon-colombia/). The ASPS [briefing paper on cosmetic surgery tourism](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/news/briefing-papers/briefing-paper-cosmetic-surgery-tourism) gives the same advice for any trip: verify credentials and plan follow-up before you travel.

## The trip is part of the budget

The surgery quote is one part of what you spend. A BBL changes the stay in two specific ways:

- **Sitting.** ASPS advises avoiding prolonged sitting for about two weeks after gluteal fat grafting. Some surgeons prefer longer. A long flight works against that.
- **The airline.** Avianca, Colombia's flag carrier, lists 10 days after buttock fat injection, 14 days after liposuction and 15 days when two or more body procedures are combined, and asks for a medical document issued no more than 10 days before travel. A BBL includes liposuction, so ask which procedures that document will name.

Budget lodging and a changeable return ticket for that stay. Day-by-day planning is in [how long to stay after a BBL](/blog/bbl-colombia/) and [how many days before flying](/blog/flying-after-plastic-surgery/).

## Frequently asked questions

**How much does a BBL cost in Colombia?**
There is no published surgery price. The plan is quoted after a valuation because the donor areas, the amount of fat and any combined procedure change the operation.

**What does a BBL quote include?**
Ask it to name the surgeon's work, anesthesia, the clinic or hospital, lab tests, garments, medication, follow-up visits and any prescribed post-op care. If a line is missing, treat it as not included until it is confirmed in writing.

**What changes the cost of a BBL?**
The donor areas, how much fat can be moved, whether another procedure is combined, the safety steps the plan uses, and the tests your surgeon asks for before surgery.

**How do I compare BBL quotes?**
Match the areas and the grafting sites, ask where the fat will be placed and how that plane is confirmed, and line up anesthesia, the facility, tests and follow-up. Then verify the surgeon in ReTHUS and the facility's authorization for surgery.

**How much is the consultation?**
USD 150 / COP 400,000, virtual or in person. It is not a surgery price.

**How long should I plan to stay after a BBL?**
ASPS advises avoiding prolonged sitting for about two weeks. Avianca lists 10 days after buttock fat injection, 14 days after liposuction and 15 days when body procedures are combined. Your surgeon confirms the date after examining you.
