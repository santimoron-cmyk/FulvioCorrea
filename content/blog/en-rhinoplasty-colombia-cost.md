## What moves a nose job quote

Mayo Clinic puts it plainly: the cost of a rhinoplasty depends on the complexity of the surgery, the surgeon's training and experience, and geography. Inside one practice, geography is fixed, so complexity does most of the work. In practice that means four questions.

**1. Is the goal cosmetic, functional, or both?** A crooked septum that blocks airflow can be straightened in the same operation, which is often called septorhinoplasty. Mayo notes that a septoplasty is often done at the same time as a rhinoplasty. Breathing work and shape work are planned together, so the consultation has to establish which one you need.

**2. How much structure has to change?** Mayo describes rhinoplasty as surgery that can change bone, cartilage, skin or all three. For small changes, cartilage may come from deeper inside the nose or from the ear; for larger changes, from a rib, implants or bone from elsewhere in the body. A larger change means a longer operation.

**3. Is this a first operation or a revision?** A nose that has been operated on before has scar tissue and less predictable structure. Mayo advises waiting at least a year before a second surgery, because the nose keeps changing as swelling settles. If you have had nasal surgery before, say so at the first contact and send the details.

**4. Is anything else planned in the same session?** Eyelids or a facelift in the same trip changes operating time, anesthesia and how long you stay in Cartagena, and the plan is quoted as one.

What usually does not belong in that list is a brand name for a device. If a quote names a specific technique or instrument, for example an "ultrasonic" rhinoplasty, ask what it changes in your plan, your recovery and your price, and get the answer in writing.

## What the U.S. figure measures, and what it leaves out

The American Society of Plastic Surgeons (ASPS) publishes surgeon/physician fees reported by its member surgeons, and it discusses anesthesia costs and operating-room fees separately.

So a U.S. surgeon-fee headline and a written quote in Colombia are not the same kind of bill. A surgeon's fee leaves out anesthesia, the facility, tests and follow-up. This page does not publish those U.S. figures and does not claim a percentage saving. ## Seven lines a written rhinoplasty quote should spell out

If a line is not written in the quote, treat it as not included until someone confirms it in writing.

| Line in the quote | What to ask |
|---|---|
| Procedure | Cosmetic, septum, or both? Primary or revision? Open or closed approach? |
| Surgeon's fee | Is it stated separately or as part of a total? |
| Anesthesia | Mayo notes rhinoplasty is done with local anesthesia plus sedation or with general anesthesia, depending on complexity. Which type is planned, who provides it, and is it included? |
| Facility | Where exactly is the surgery done, and is it authorized (*habilitada*) for surgery? |
| Tests before surgery | Which labs or studies, and are they in the quote? |
| Splint, medication and visits in Cartagena | Mayo describes an external splint for about one week. Is the visit when it comes off included, and how many other visits happen before you fly? |
| Follow-up from home | How are check-ins handled once you are back, and what happens if a small revision is ever needed? |

Add one more line that is easy to forget: the currency, and for peso quotes, the exchange-rate date.

## The trip is part of the cost

The surgery quote is one part of the budget. The other part is how many nights you stay, and for rhinoplasty the sources give clear floors:

- **ASPS and the CDC** suggest waiting 7 to 10 days before flying after facial procedures, rhinoplasty included.
- **Avianca**, Colombia's flag carrier, permits travel 10 days after rhinoplasty or septoplasty, lists cosmetic facial surgery at 14 days and a facelift or two or more facial procedures combined at 15, and asks for a medical certificate issued no more than 10 days before the flight.

Count from the surgery date, add the days before surgery for the in-person evaluation and tests, and keep the return ticket changeable. Lodging, meals and local transport for those nights belong in the same spreadsheet as the quote. The day-by-day plan, Cartagena's sun rules and what to pack are in [what to decide before a nose job trip](/blog/rhinoplasty-colombia/), and the airline table is in [flying after plastic surgery](/blog/flying-after-plastic-surgery/).

One cost that does not show up in any quote is time at home. ASPS notes that the contour of the nose can take up to a year to refine, so the final result is judged long after you fly back. Ask, before you pay anything, how follow-up from abroad works.

## A low quote is a question, not an answer

A much lower number for what looks like the same plan usually means the plan is not the same. Check:

1. **Who operates.** Mayo Clinic notes that most rhinoplasties are done by plastic surgeons, facial plastic surgeons or ENT surgeons, and suggests a surgeon who does rhinoplasty often. In Colombia, check the [ReTHUS registry](https://www.minsalud.gov.co/salud/PO/paginas/registro-unico-nacional-del-talento-humano-en-salud-rethus.aspx) and, if membership is claimed, the [SCCP surgeon search](https://cirugiaplastica.org.co/buscar-cirujano/).
2. **Where.** Ask for the facility's name and address in writing; it is often not the consultation address.
3. **What is missing.** Anesthesia, the facility, tests or follow-up may simply not be in the cheaper number.
