## What changes the cost of a rhinoplasty

Four questions explain most of the difference between one nose-job quote and another.

**1. Is the goal cosmetic, functional, or both?** A crooked septum that blocks airflow can be straightened in the same operation. That plan is often called a septorhinoplasty. Mayo Clinic notes that a septoplasty is often done at the same time as a rhinoplasty. Breathing and shape are planned together, so the valuation has to establish which one you need.

**2. How much structure has to change?** Mayo Clinic describes rhinoplasty as surgery that can change bone, cartilage, skin or all three. For small changes, cartilage may come from deeper inside the nose or from the ear. For larger changes, it may come from a rib, from an implant or from bone elsewhere in the body. A larger change is a longer operation.

**3. Is this a first operation or a revision?** A nose that has already been operated on has internal scar and less predictable structure. Mayo Clinic advises waiting at least a year before a second surgery, because the nose keeps changing as swelling settles. If you have had nasal surgery before, say so at the first contact and send the operative details.

**4. Is anything else planned in the same session?** Eyelids or a facelift on the same trip change operating time, anesthesia and how many days you stay in Cartagena. The combination is quoted as one plan, not by adding separate quotes. See [rhinoplasty in Cartagena](/procedures/rhinoplasty/).

A device name does not belong on that list by itself. If a quote mentions a specific technique or instrument, for example an ultrasonic rhinoplasty, ask what it changes in your plan, your recovery and the written quote, and get the answer in writing.

The American Society of Plastic Surgeons (ASPS) publishes surgeon fees reported by its members and discusses anesthesia and operating-room fees separately. A surgeon's fee is not a total bill. This page does not reprint those U.S. figures and does not claim a percentage saving. Compare a full written quote from home with a full written quote here, then add the trip.

## What the quote includes

A useful rhinoplasty quote names each service. If a line is not written down, treat it as not included until someone confirms it in writing.

| Item | What the line should say |
|---|---|
| Procedure | Cosmetic, septum, or both. Primary or revision. Open or closed approach |
| Surgeon | Whether the surgeon's fee is stated on its own or inside a total |
| Anesthesia | Mayo Clinic notes local anesthesia with sedation or general anesthesia, depending on complexity. Which type is planned, who provides it, and whether that fee is in the quote |
| Clinic or hospital | Where the surgery is done, and whether the facility is authorized for surgery |
| Lab tests | Which studies are required, and whether they are in the quote |
| Splint and medication | Mayo Clinic describes an external splint for about one week. Whether the visit when it comes off is included, and what medication is provided |
| Follow-up visits | How many visits happen in Cartagena before you fly, and how check-ins work after you are home |
| Post-op care | What happens if a small revision is ever discussed later, and how follow-up from abroad is handled |
| Currency | USD or COP, and the exchange-rate date if the quote is in pesos |

Flights, lodging, meals and time away from work sit outside the surgical quote. One cost that no quote lists is time at home: ASPS notes that the contour of the nose can take up to a year to refine, so the result is judged long after the flight back.

## How to compare quotes and red flags

A much lower number for what looks like the same nose usually means the plan is not the same. Before you compare:

1. **Same operation.** Cosmetic only, septum as well, primary or revision, and any other facial procedure in the same session.
2. **Who operates.** Mayo Clinic notes that most rhinoplasties are done by plastic surgeons, facial plastic surgeons or ear, nose and throat surgeons, and suggests someone who does rhinoplasty often. In Colombia, check the [ReTHUS registry](https://www.minsalud.gov.co/salud/PO/paginas/registro-unico-nacional-del-talento-humano-en-salud-rethus.aspx). If membership is claimed, use the [SCCP surgeon search](https://cirugiaplastica.org.co/buscar-cirujano/).
3. **Where.** The facility's name and address in writing. It is often not the consultation address. Ask whether it is authorized for surgical services.
4. **Missing lines.** Anesthesia, the facility, tests, the splint visit or follow-up left out of the cheaper quote.
5. **Follow-up after you fly.** Ask, before you pay, how check-ins work from home and what happens if you need to be seen again.
6. **Pressure to decide from a headline.** A single number with no itemized lines is not a quote you can compare.

The longer checklist is in [how to choose a plastic surgeon in Colombia](/best-plastic-surgeon-colombia/). The ASPS [briefing paper on cosmetic surgery tourism](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/news/briefing-papers/briefing-paper-cosmetic-surgery-tourism) and the CDC [Yellow Book note on medical tourism](https://www.cdc.gov/yellow-book/hcp/health-care-abroad/medical-tourism.html) both say to verify credentials and plan follow-up before you travel.

## The trip is part of the budget

- **ASPS and the CDC** suggest waiting 7 to 10 days before flying after facial procedures, rhinoplasty included.
- **Avianca**, Colombia's flag carrier, permits travel 10 days after rhinoplasty or septoplasty, lists cosmetic facial surgery at 14 days, and a facelift or two or more facial procedures combined at 15 days. It asks for a medical document issued no more than 10 days before the flight.

Count from the surgery date, add the days before surgery for the in-person evaluation and tests, and keep the return ticket changeable. ## Frequently asked questions

**How much does a rhinoplasty cost in Colombia?**
There is no published surgery price. The plan is quoted after a valuation because it depends on whether the goal is shape, breathing or both, how much structure has to change, and whether this is a first operation or a revision.

**What does a rhinoplasty quote include?**
Ask it to name the procedure, the surgeon's fee, anesthesia, the clinic or hospital, lab tests, the splint and medication, and the follow-up visits in Cartagena and after you fly home.

**Does straightening the septum change the quote?**
It can. Correcting a deviated septum in the same operation changes what is done. The valuation decides whether the plan is cosmetic, functional or both, and the written quote reflects that.

**How do I compare rhinoplasty quotes?**
Match primary versus revision and whether the septum is included, then line up anesthesia, the facility, tests and follow-up. Verify the surgeon in ReTHUS and ask for the facility's name and address in writing.

**How much is the consultation?**
USD 150 / COP 400,000, virtual or in person. It is not a surgery price.

**How many days should I budget in Cartagena?**
At least 10 days after surgery if you fly Avianca, plus the days before surgery. ASPS and the CDC suggest 7 to 10 days after facial procedures. Your surgeon confirms the date after examining you.
