## What changes the cost of lipo 360

The same name covers different operations. Mayo Clinic notes that the risk of complications rises when the surgeon works on larger body surfaces or does several procedures in the same operation, and that large-volume liposuction can shift enough fluid to affect the kidneys, heart and lungs. More area means more operating time and more planning.

- **Which areas.** Abdomen and flanks only, or abdomen, flanks, upper back and lower back. Arms or legs added to the plan change operating time again.
- **Which technique.** Mayo Clinic describes suction-assisted liposuction, the most common type, along with ultrasound-assisted, laser-assisted and power-assisted techniques. The surgeon selects the technique from your goals, the area and any previous liposuction. No single device is the right choice for everyone.
- **Definition or volume.** HD liposculpture (listed here as Liposurg) and a volume-only lipo 360 are different plans. Each one is quoted on its own after the consultation.
- **Fat transfer.** Once fat is grafted to the buttocks, the plan is a Brazilian butt lift, with its own technique and safety rules. It is quoted per case. See [what a BBL quote includes](/blog/bbl-colombia-cost/).
- **Your skin.** Liposuction does not tighten loose skin. If the consultation finds loose lower-abdominal skin or a stretched abdominal wall, the conversation moves toward [tummy tuck or liposuction](/blog/tummy-tuck-vs-liposuction/) or a lipoabdominoplasty, which is a different operation and is quoted on its own.

So the honest answer to "how much is lipo 360 in Colombia?" is a written quote after the consultation.

## What the quote includes

A surgical bill is made of separate services. A useful quote names each one so you can compare like with like. If a line is missing, treat it as not included until someone confirms it in writing.

| Item | What the line should say |
|---|---|
| Surgeon | The areas agreed at the consultation, and whether the plan is HD liposculpture or volume reduction |
| Anesthesia | Mayo Clinic explains that some liposuction uses local or regional anesthesia plus sedation and some uses general anesthesia. Who the anesthesiologist is, and whether that fee is in the quote |
| Clinic or hospital | The operating room and, if the plan needs it, an overnight stay. Mayo Clinic notes that a large amount of fat, or other procedures at the same time, may move surgery to a hospital |
| Lab tests | Which studies are required, and whether they are in the quote |
| Garments | Whether the first compression garment is included, and whether you will need another size as swelling goes down |
| Medication | What is provided for the first days |
| Follow-up visits | How many visits happen in Cartagena, and how follow-up works after you fly home |
| Post-op care | Massage or lymphatic drainage only if it is prescribed, and whether it is in the quote or arranged separately |
| Currency | USD or COP, and the exchange-rate date if the quote is in pesos |

Flights, lodging, meals, a companion and time away from work sit outside the surgical quote. Some patients arrange an equipped [recovery house in Cartagena](/recovery-house-cartagena/) with 24/7 nursing, transfers and supplies. That plan is set at the valuation and has no package price.

## How to compare quotes and red flags

A lower number is only a better comparison if it describes the same operation.

1. **Same areas.** Abdomen, flanks, upper back, lower back, and any arms or legs.
2. **Same goal.** HD liposculpture or volume reduction, and whether fat transfer is included.
3. **Same lines.** Surgeon, anesthesia, facility, tests, garment, medication and follow-up, side by side.
4. **Currency.** COP quotes should name the exchange-rate date.
5. **Who operates.** In Colombia, check the plastic surgery registration in [ReTHUS](https://www.minsalud.gov.co/salud/PO/paginas/registro-unico-nacional-del-talento-humano-en-salud-rethus.aspx). If membership is claimed, check the [Colombian Society of Plastic Surgery (SCCP)](https://cirugiaplastica.org.co/). Ask whether the facility is authorized for surgical services (REPS).
6. **If you need another night.** Ask what happens, and who is responsible, if you need an extra visit before you are cleared to fly.

A quote that promises liposuction will remove cellulite or stretch marks should be questioned. Mayo Clinic states that liposuction does not treat those, and that it is not a weight-loss method. The ASPS [briefing paper on cosmetic surgery tourism](https://www.plasticsurgery.org/news/briefing-papers/briefing-paper-cosmetic-surgery-tourism) says to verify credentials and plan follow-up before traveling. What to bring to the consultation is in [planning liposuction in Cartagena](/blog/liposuction-cartagena/).

## The trip is part of the budget

The American Society of Plastic Surgeons (ASPS) suggests waiting five to seven days after body procedures such as liposuction before flying. Avianca, Colombia's flag carrier, lists 14 days after liposuction and 15 days when two or more body procedures are combined, and asks for a medical document issued no more than 10 days before the flight. Your surgeon sets the date. Budget lodging with a few days of margin. The week-by-week plan is in [liposuction recovery](/blog/liposuction-recovery/).
